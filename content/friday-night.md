## Activities

### Brainteaser Battles — Havemeyer 209, 309

*Presented by Susquehanna.* Work in teams through puzzles designed by our traders to test quantitative reasoning and out-of-the-box thinking. The winning team receives Susquehanna-branded poker sets!

### Integration Bee Prequalifiers — Uris 301

Compete on Mentimeter and catch the start of the Top 16 bracket leading up to Saturday's Top 8 showdown! Integrals by Alex Yang, author of *More Unimaginable and Speedy Integration Techniques*.

### Poker Night — Lerner Broadway Room

*Presented by Columbia Quant Group.* Open to all levels. First 80 attendees, first-come, first-served. Snacks served.

### Board Game Night — Lerner 510, 515

*Presented by Columbia Games Club.* Catan, Coup, Avalon, Sheriff of Nottingham, and more. Open to all levels. Snacks will be served.

### Smash Night — Lerner 478

*Presented by Columbia Smash & FGC.* Smash Bros Ultimate, Street Fighter 6, Guilty Gear: Strive, and more! BYO controller. Open to all levels. First 40 attendees.
